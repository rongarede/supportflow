# refund-eligibility
A verified duplicate charge may be submitted as a refund request when its order identifier, amount, and currency match the billing record.

# refund-exclusion
Do not auto-submit a refund request when an exclusion may apply. Resolve the exclusion with a human reviewer before taking an action.
